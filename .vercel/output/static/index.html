Symptoms

Low online visibility

01 / 07

What we see

The business does not appear in search results or AI-generated answers for relevant queries.

The question

Is the gap in SEO, GEO, or brand awareness?

First move

Audit search rankings and AI tool citations. Build a combined SEO + GEO content strategy.

Diagnosis framework. Not a client result.

Low conversion rate

02 / 07

What we see

Traffic is growing but leads and sales are not following.

The question

Is the issue the website, the offer, or the funnel?

First move

Review the site's landing pages, CTAs, and lead capture. Run a CRO sprint before scaling spend.

Diagnosis framework. Not a client result.

Poor lead quality

03 / 07

What we see

Leads are coming in but most don't convert to clients.

The question

Are we targeting the wrong audience or converting the wrong signals?

First move

Review ICP fit, channel attribution, and CRM data before the next campaign.

Diagnosis framework. Not a client result.

Manual operations slowing growth

04 / 07

What we see

The team spends time on tasks that could be automated, like follow-ups, reporting, and data entry.

The question

Which workflows have the highest automation ROI?

First move

Map the top five manual tasks and implement no-code automations for the highest-volume ones first.

Diagnosis framework. Not a client result.

Weak brand positioning

05 / 07

What we see

The business is hard to describe in one sentence and loses to better-known competitors.

The question

Is the gap in messaging, visual identity, or distribution?

First move

Run a brand audit and position the business around one clear outcome it delivers.

Diagnosis framework. Not a client result.

No data visibility

06 / 07

What we see

Key metrics like CAC, LTV, margin and pipeline are tracked across spreadsheets or not at all.

The question

What decisions are we making blind that a dashboard would fix?

First move

Identify three decisions made monthly without data. Build a BI view for those first.

Diagnosis framework. Not a client result.

Growth has stalled

07 / 07

What we see

Revenue or leads have plateaued after a period of growth. The team is not sure why.

The question

Is the stall in acquisition, retention, or operations?

First move

Compare the last growth period to now. Find the metric that changed first and trace it back.
